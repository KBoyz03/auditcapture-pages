AuditCapture Support

AuditCapture records safety, environmental, quality and facility findings at a workplace: photograph what you found, add the details, assign an owner, and track it through to close-out.

Get help

Email auditcapture@gmail.com and you will get a reply within two working days. It helps to include:

  • What you were doing when it went wrong
  • The finding ID, if it was about a particular finding (for example AV-SAF-000002)
  • Your phone and its version — iPhone or Android

Common questions

I have no signal at the site. Will I lose the finding?

No. Findings and photos save on your phone and upload by themselves once there is a connection. Home shows how many are waiting.

Can I fix a finding after saving it?

Yes. Open it and tap Edit. Moving a due date asks you for a reason, which is kept with the finding so the history stays honest.

Who can see what I record?

People in your organisation, for the sites they have access to. Nobody outside it. See the privacy policy for the detail.

Someone else closed my finding.

Anyone at the site can close or reopen a finding, and the person who reported it is notified when somebody else closes it. The finding records who closed it and when.

How do I get my data out?

The Data tab exports every finding to Excel, with each chart on its own tab and the numbers behind it alongside. Charts can also be exported as images, PowerPoint slides or a PDF.

I want my account deleted.

Email auditcapture@gmail.com and it will be removed.
